Steady states and guesses

A transient in this library starts from an operating point, and an operating point is a root of the system’s equations with every time derivative at zero. This page explains why, what a steady solve needs from its guess, what the library builds of it, and what the two steps of the solve do. Get a steady solve to converge does the task, and Building the Pool Reactor Loop seeds a full pool reactor by hand.

Why a transient starts from a steady state

Started anywhere other than an operating point, a transient first shows the plant relaxing toward its own steady state, mixed into the response to the change it was meant to show. The steady state also gives the transient a consistent start: the differential rows of the initial vector, the coolant and plate temperatures and, on a loop with inertia, the flows, are where IDA begins, and by default it makes only the algebraic rows and the derivatives consistent with them, so a differential row that is off is kept as given.

What a guess has to get right

A steady solve is a Newton-type iteration, and it converges from a starting point close enough to the root. Most of the vector is cheap to get close: a pressure follows from a flow through each component’s own law, a coolant temperature from the power, the flow and the heat capacity, a wall coefficient from the wall law at a guessed temperature. The flows are the part that has to be right, because every other row is computed from them, and of the flows the sign matters most. A guess with the water going the wrong way through a heated channel feeds the channel from the wrong end and sets its buoyancy against the flow it should drive. The natural-circulation loop of the how-to fails from a forced-flow guess and converges from two upward guesses that differ threefold in magnitude: there the magnitude does not matter and the sign does.

What the library builds and what it does not

guess_steady_state builds the hydraulic part from flows it is given. It needs a flow on one component of every edge and raises MissingFlowError naming the edges without one: it does not complete the flows from the junction balances, and it does not choose a sign. From those flows and one temperature it computes each component’s pressure, sets the temperatures uniform, evaluates the wall coefficients of each wall-coupled channel and adds up the absolute pressures from the reference. A pump that imposes its flow, or a closed flapper, has no pressure that follows from the flow, and its guess is zero. symmetric_plate_steady_state solves a plate and its channel at a given flow, and point_kinetics_steady_state puts the kinetics at a given power and zero reactivity; merge lets the later seed win where two overlap. The flows and their signs are what one brings.

The two steps of a steady solve

solve_steady first calls scipy’s root with the options it is given. When root reports that it did not converge, the library raises an AlgRuntimeError, and with the default globalize="auto" that error, and only that one, sends the solve on to scaled_newton; any other exception from scipy propagates. globalize=True skips scipy and globalize=False lets the error propagate. The fallback exists for guesses far from the root, where the globalisation of scipy’s default method stalls.

scaled_newton is Newton’s method with a line search: each step is halved until the norm of the residual falls. The damping is what makes it converge. Each linear solve is equilibrated, rows then columns, as insurance for a badly scaled system, pressures of about 10⁵ Pa beside temperatures of about 100 °C, and a small Tikhonov term keeps a rank-deficient step finite. It stops when the residual norm falls below 1e-6, and raises after 100 iterations or when no step decreases the residual, with the best iterate attached as the error’s y for diagnosis. scales sets the nominal magnitudes behind its finite-difference step and is read only when no jac is passed; with a jac both steps use it.

A failure of both steps says the guess is outside the basin or the equations have no root there. A wrong flow sign is the first suspect. A missing edge flow fails earlier, at the guess; check_gravity_mismatch finds a loop whose heights do not close at zero flow; Diagnose a failing solve goes through them, and Natural circulation and flow reversal explains why a natural-circulation loop is the case where the sign decides.
