Examples

The examples are meant to be read in the order below, from a loop of three components to a pool reactor through a loss of flow.

From a first loop to a fuel rod

  • A first loop builds a pump, a pipe and a cooler into a loop, solves its steady state and checks the flow by hand.

  • Series RLC Circuit writes the resistor, the inductor and the capacitor as three Calculation classes of its own and solves the circuit in time.

  • The Cube Problem wires twelve resistors on the edges of a cube with junctions and recovers the cube’s equivalent resistance.

  • Primary Cooling System Coastdown adds a flywheel to a pump and a resistor and compares the coastdown transient with its analytic solution.

  • Plates and Channels, an MTR Love Story couples a fuel plate to the coolant channel that cools it.

  • Multiple Plates In a Fuel Rod stacks such plates and channels into a rod and solves it in a loop with a pump and a heat exchanger.

More calculations

  • Coupled Masses and Springs writes one calculation for a mass between two springs and couples two of them.

  • Planar Pendulum writes a pendulum as one calculation with an algebraic constraint for its string.

Loss of flow

A pool reactor assembled step by step from the library’s components, shipped as the examples package, then taken from its operating point through a loss of flow into natural circulation.

  • Building the Pool Reactor Loop puts the reactor together one part at a time and solves a first steady state.

  • A Pool Reactor for Loss-of-Flow Analysis solves the operating point and checks it against hand estimates.

  • Both Pumps Trip: Coastdown, Trip, Flapper, Natural Convection follows both pumps tripping, through the reactor trip and the flapper opening, into natural circulation.

  • One Pump, a Stuck Flapper, and How Far to Trust the Numbers trips one pump, keeps the flapper shut, and measures how far the reported times and temperatures can be trusted.
