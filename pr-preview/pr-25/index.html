STREAM: System Thermohydraulics for Reactor Evaluation, Analysis & Modeling

STREAM builds and solves systems of coupled differential algebraic equations, \(M\dot{y} = F(y, t)\), put together from calculations that each own a slice of the state vector \(y\). It ships thermal-hydraulic components, from pumps and channels to fuel plates and point kinetics, with the correlations behind them, for modelling the heat removal of research reactors.

Conventions

The library holds to a few conventions throughout; Sign conventions collects the signs.

  • Temperatures are in °C: the property functions of light_water take °C.

  • Absolute pressure is in pascals, wherever p_abs is routed to a component, such as a heated channel, through abs_pressure_comps on FlowGraph.

  • A component’s pressure is the gain from its inlet to its outlet: a pump’s head is positive, a loss is negative, and the gains cancel around every loop.

  • A flow is positive along its drawn edge, from the first junction of flow_edge’s (u, v) to the second.

  • A Gravity’s disposition is positive when the outlet is below the inlet, so a riser takes a negative one.

  • A plate and its channel share the same z_boundaries, as Build a plate, an assembly or a rod states.
